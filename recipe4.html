Onigiri Rice Balls

Japanese-style rice balls shaped by hand, with a tasty filling hidden in the middle.

Serves 1 • 3 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of warm cooked short-grain rice
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 tablespoons of filling, such as canned tuna or cooked chicken
  • 1 sheet of roasted seaweed, cut into strips

Equipment

  • Small bowl of water
  • Plate
  • Plastic wrap (optional)
  • Spoon

Instructions

  1. Wet your hands with water and rub a pinch of salt on your palms. This keeps the rice from sticking.
  2. Scoop about half of the rice into your hand and press a small hole in the middle.
  3. Add the filling, then cover with a little more rice and press into a triangle or ball.
  4. Wrap a strip of seaweed around the bottom and serve. Rice that is still warm shapes best, so let it cool until it is comfortable to touch.

Ingredient Notes

Seaweed, fish fillings, and soy-based fillings can be allergens. Eat soon after making, and refrigerate any leftovers within 2 hours.

Make It Your Own

Mix in sesame seeds, or try a different filling such as cooked salmon or avocado.
