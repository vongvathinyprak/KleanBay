Burger Bowl

A simple burger bowl made with ground beef, avocado, lettuce, tomato, and a special sauce.

Serves 1 • 5 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • Half an avocado
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • Half a tomato
  • Special sauce

Equipment

  • Bowl
  • Fork

Instructions

  1. Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat until browned and cooked through.
  2. Gather the lettuce, tomato, and avocado and chop them into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Put the ingredients in a bowl.
  4. Add the special sauce on top.
  5. Enjoy your burger bowl!

Make It Your Own

Try a different ingredient combination or add toppings like cheese or pickles.
