Peanut Butter Banana Toast

Crisp toast topped with peanut butter and fresh banana slices.

Serves 1 • 3 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tablespoons of peanut butter
  • 1 banana, peeled and sliced
  • A pinch of cinnamon, optional

Equipment

  • Toaster
  • Plate
  • Spreading knife

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread to your preferred level using your toaster's instructions.
  2. Place the toast on a plate and spread peanut butter over each slice.
  3. Arrange the banana slices on top.
  4. Add a light sprinkle of cinnamon, if you like, and serve.

Ingredient Notes

This recipe uses peanuts and bread. Check all ingredient labels for allergens and choose products suitable for your needs.

Make It Your Own

Prefer a different texture? Mash the banana before adding it to the toast.
