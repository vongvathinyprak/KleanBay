Oreo Mug Cake

A simple mug cake made with crushed Oreos, milk, and a microwave (or airfryer).

Serves 1 • 3 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 Oreo cookies, crushed
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • Chocolate bar

Equipment

  • Bowl
  • Fork
  • Measuring cup
  • Microwave or airfryer

Instructions

  1. Combine the crushed Oreos and milk in a bowl.
  2. Stir the mixture until well combined.
  3. Put chocolate bar in the middle of the mixture.
  4. Place the bowl in the microwave or airfryer and cook for 5-8 minutes on 150Degrees, or until the cake has risen and is cooked through.
  5. Let the cake cool for a few minutes before serving.
  6. Enjoy your Oreo mug cake!
