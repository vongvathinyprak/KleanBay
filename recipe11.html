Honey Garlic Chicken

Juicy bite-size chicken pieces coated in a sticky, sweet and savory honey garlic sauce.

Serves 2 • 5 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 300 grams of boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 3 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons of honey
  • 2 tablespoons of soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon of cooking oil

Equipment

  • Non-stick pan
  • Spatula or wooden spoon
  • Small bowl
  • Cutting board and knife

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, stir the honey and soy sauce together. Set the sauce aside.
  2. Heat the oil in the pan on medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and cooked all the way through. Ask an adult to help with the stove.
  3. Turn the heat down to medium-low. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds until it smells fragrant. Do not let it burn.
  4. Pour in the sauce and stir for 2 to 3 minutes until it bubbles and thickens into a glaze that coats the chicken.
  5. Check that there is no pink inside the chicken. It should reach 74°C (165°F) if you have a thermometer. Serve over warm rice and top with green onion or sesame seeds.

Ingredient Notes

Contains soy and wheat (in most soy sauces). Honey is not suitable for babies under 1 year old. Wash your hands, the cutting board, and the knife after touching raw chicken.

Make It Your Own

Add a pinch of chili flakes, a squeeze of lime, or broccoli cooked in the same pan.
