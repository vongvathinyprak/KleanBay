About Klean Bay

A student-built recipe website focused on simple ingredients and clear instructions.

The Problem We Want to Solve

Our project focuses on a familiar situation: wanting something to eat without following a complicated recipe.

Klean Bay brings short ingredient lists, step-by-step instructions, and recipe comparisons together in one place.
