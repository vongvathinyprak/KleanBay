Garlic Butter Noodles

Glossy noodles tossed in garlic, butter, and soy sauce. It is a full meal in about 10 minutes.

Serves 1 • 4 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 100 grams of spaghetti or instant noodles
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce

Equipment

  • Pot
  • Pan
  • Colander
  • Spoon

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles following the package directions, then drain. Keep a spoonful of the cooking water.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan on low heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 to 60 seconds, stirring, until it smells fragrant. Do not let it turn brown.
  3. Add the noodles, soy sauce, and a spoonful of cooking water. Toss for 1 minute until coated.
  4. Top with green onion, sesame seeds, or a soft-cooked egg if you like.

Ingredient Notes

Contains wheat, milk, and soy. Ask an adult to help with the stove and boiling water. Garlic burns quickly, so keep the heat low.

Make It Your Own

Add parmesan, chili flakes, or a squeeze of lemon.
