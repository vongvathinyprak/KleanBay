Crepe Rolls

A simple crepe rolls made with crepes, filling, and nutella.

Serves 1 • 5 main ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • Nutella
  • Oil

Equipment

  • Pan
  • Mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Spatula

Instructions

  1. Mix the eggs, milk, and flour in a mixing bowl until smooth.
  2. Preheat a pan over medium heat and lightly oil it.
  3. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan and swirl to coat the bottom evenly.
  4. Cook for 1-2 minutes until the edges start to lift, then flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add the nutella on top.
  6. Enjoy your Crepe Rolls!

Make It Your Own

Try a different ingredient combination or add toppings like strawberries or whipped cream.
